## Relevance and reading focus

Complex-structure prediction and confidence reference. Use it to interpret structure confidence and distinguish this from direct affinity prediction. The reported affinity task concerns small-molecule–protein interactions; do not interpret a VHH–PD-L1 ipTM score as a measured or validated Kd.

## Questions to answer while reading

- Which confidence metrics concern local structure versus interchain placement?
- What input templates, constraints and sampling settings were used?
- Which outputs are valid for a protein–protein complex?
